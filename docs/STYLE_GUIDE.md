# CustodyBuddy Visual System

## Current Direction

CustodyBuddy uses a premium legal-tech editorial system.

### Background
- Dark navy: `#020716`
- Secondary navy: `#07152B`
- Cool blue haze: `#12345B`
- Faint low-contrast technology grid
- Optional sophisticated office setting with deep navy drapes, dark stone desk, softly blurred legal books, plant, lamp, or scales in the distance
- No galaxy, star-field, orbital, or celestial background treatment in current production assets

### Type
- Headlines: elegant serif, high contrast
- Body: clean sans serif
- Headline emphasis: 1–3 words in celestial gold and italic when appropriate
- Warm white: `#F7F3EA`
- Body text: `#D6DCE8`

### Gold
Celestial gold `#F6BA21` is for selected headline words, icons, dividers, borders, slide numbers, and small object details only. Do not use gold haze, large gold fields, or gold background gradients.

### Illustration DNA
- Editorial 3D still-life
- Matte navy leather and paper folders
- Warm cream paper
- Small restrained brushed-gold details
- Realistic depth, soft directional lighting, tactile materials
- Legal-tech objects: folders, documents, checklists, message cards, timelines, calendars, magnifying glasses, scales, pens
- Calm, organized, credible
- No people by default
- No angry couples, courtroom drama, warning-red graphics, chains, broken hearts, or robots

### Layout
- One primary visual scene
- Asymmetrical composition
- Generous negative space for accessible text
- High contrast and readable at mobile size
- Important content kept away from crop edges

## Production Rule
Current production backgrounds are legal-tech grid and/or premium navy office with drapes. Older celestial/orbital assets are retained only in the archive for historical reference.
